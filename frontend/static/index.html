    One thing first

    How a run works

    Five agents, in order. Each hands the next only what it could actually show.

    1. 01Validate Checks your answers make sense together, and asks you about it when two of them disagree.
    2. 02Research Reads current news for the parts of the market you named, and keeps what it can cite.
    3. 03Screen Finds listed companies genuinely exposed to those themes, and says why it dropped the rest.
    4. 04Critique Retrieves the bear case and argues against every candidate before any of them survives.
    5. 05Write Writes up to three of them — or recommends nothing, and says why.

    Runs it has already made

    What this is

    A five-agent research pipeline. It validates what you tell it, reads current news for the sectors you name, finds listed companies genuinely exposed to what it found, retrieves the bear case and attacks each candidate, then writes up to three of them — or recommends nothing, and says why.

    Every claim it makes cites either an article it actually retrieved or a figure it actually measured. It cannot cite anything else: the schema has nowhere to put one.

    What it will not do

    It will not tell you how much to buy. Dividing your money three ways is two lines of code, and it is the point where research becomes advice, so there is no field for it. It will not give you a price target or a sell date either — it has no valuation model, and inventing that number would be the only figure in the output citing nothing. What it gives you instead is a list of things that would mean the idea has stopped working.

    Limits worth knowing

    It runs on free API tiers, which is roughly seven runs a day shared by everyone who visits. When they are gone, the recorded runs above are what the site can show. A run can also legitimately recommend nothing.
